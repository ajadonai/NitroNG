# Brief: 9 Comparison Posts — Nitro NG vs Nigerian SMM Competitors

## What this is

9 blog posts, one per competitor. Each post targets the query "[Competitor name] vs Nitro" or "is [Competitor] better than Nitro" and similar comparison searches. These are the other 9 panels in Google's current top-10 Nigerian SMM panel ranking.

## Tone

Honest, not aggressive. The Nitro brand voice is: we tell you the truth even when it makes us look worse. Do not bash competitors. Do not make claims about competitors you cannot verify from their public website. Acknowledge where a competitor might genuinely be better. The reader should finish the post trusting Nitro more because we were fair, not because we trashed the other panel.

If you cannot find real, verifiable information about a competitor from their public site, say so explicitly: "We could not verify [claim] from their website." Do not invent features or pricing for competitors.

## Post structure (same for all 9)

Each post should be 800-1,200 words and follow this structure:

### 1. Opening (2-3 sentences)
What both panels are, who this comparison is for. No filler.

### 2. What [Competitor] offers
Based only on what is publicly visible on their website. If they list pricing, quote it. If they don't, say "pricing is not publicly listed." If their site is down or empty, say so. Do not guess.

### 3. What Nitro NG offers
Use the real data below. Be factual, not salesy.

### 4. Head-to-head comparison
A markdown table comparing 5-7 dimensions:
- Platforms supported
- Starting prices (cheapest service)
- Nigerian-targeted services
- Payment methods
- Refill guarantee
- Minimum deposit
- Account required

### 5. Where [Competitor] might be the better choice
Be genuinely honest. If they have a guest checkout and someone just wants one quick boost, say so. If they bundle VTU and someone wants one dashboard for everything, say so.

### 6. Where Nitro is the better choice
State it plainly based on the real advantages.

### 7. The honest take (2-3 sentences)
Summary. No hard sell. End with: "The best way to test any panel is a small order. We wrote a guide on how to do that properly: [how to test an SMM panel before you commit](/blog/how-to-test-an-smm-panel-before-you-commit)."

### 8. FAQ (3 questions)
- "Is [Competitor] legit?" — answer honestly: if you can verify they operate and deliver, say so; if you cannot, say so
- "Is [Competitor] cheaper than Nitro?" — compare what you can verify
- "Which should I use for [the thing the competitor claims to be best at]?" — honest recommendation
