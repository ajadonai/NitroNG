Travel eSIM, the first of the tools

Data abroad, bought in naira from the wallet before the flight, delivered as a QR code. Drawn as a dashboard page in the same print as the rest. Destinations, networks and prices here are examples; the real catalogue and rates come from the provider at build time.

On a computer

The page in the dashboard's content column. Tap a destination or a plan; the receipt follows.

On a phone

Same page, stacked; the install sheet rises from the bottom.

  • Where you are going comes first. A dark night-flight card with one search and the destinations Nigerians fly to most, plus regional plans for a multi-country trip. Pick one and its plans appear under it.
  • A plan is data and days, priced in naira, with the price per GB under it so the bigger plans show their value. Printed tickets like the wallet's bonus tickets, one "Most picked" stamp.
  • The receipt says what you are buying in a sentence and pays from the wallet with the shared gold button. Not enough in the wallet? The button becomes Top up the difference.
  • After paying, the QR is right there: scan it, or on an iPhone install with one tap, with the manual codes underneath and a copy in your email. It says when the days start (when it first connects abroad), so nobody burns a plan by installing early.
  • My eSIMs shows what is left: data used, days left, top up, and the ones still waiting to be installed.
  • The two honest bits are on the page: check your phone takes an eSIM (dial *#06#, look for EID), and travel only. It is data abroad, not a Nigerian line, and your WhatsApp stays on your own number.

Four calls before I write the brief

  1. Provider. The research favoured Airalo's Partner API (largest catalogue, branded delivery email), with eSIM Go as the wholesale alternative. Go with Airalo and open the partner account?
  2. Margin. Resellers typically add 15 to 30 percent. Pick a starting markup; prices would show in naira at that day's rate, rounded to the nearest ₦100, and be fixed at checkout.
  3. Where it lives. A new Tools section in the sidebar and the phone's More sheet, Travel eSIM first, with airtime, gift cards and bills joining it later. And a public /esim page for search later, or dashboard only for now?
  4. Refunds. Providers refund an eSIM only if it was never installed. Refund to the wallet on that rule, within 30 days of buying?
